# Confident LIMS

> Confident LIMS is a cloud-based Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) built for high-velocity analytical labs and their testing clients. It streamlines workflows, supports compliance, and enables fast reporting across cannabis testing, food & beverage, environmental, agricultural, nutraceutical, cosmetics, oil & gas, and industrial chemical testing industries.

- Full details for AI & LLMs: https://www.confidentlims.com/llms-full.txt

Confident LIMS combines:
- **LIMS for Labs**: sample accession → workflow → results → reporting
- **Testing Network**: client ordering, results delivery, invoicing, and B2B coordination
- **Client Platform**: ordering + results across multiple labs with exports and API integrations
- **API for Integrators**: 2-way sync for labs and 1-way sync for clients
